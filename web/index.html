Landing-site navigator

Click the map or enter a latitude/longitude (degrees, east-positive) inside the 20 km window. Each point gets its own terrain horizon (2 m observer, rays out to the window edge) and is scored over 2027 on Sun and Earth visibility. Sun and Earth directions are taken from the window centre; within 10 km that shifts them by at most about 0.3 degrees. The NASA columns are the published 18.6-year, 0 m maps, so the difference also contains the observer-height and time-span effects described on the Validation tab. Ranking: mean of Sun and Earth fraction minus a small penalty for the longest dark spell. Yellow dots: 48 px grid; blue: points you picked (*).
