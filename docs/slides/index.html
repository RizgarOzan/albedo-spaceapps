Lunar Light Clock

147 hours of darkness. On Shackleton's rim the Sun can stay below the horizon for 147 hours in a row in 2027. Where would you land?

When are the Sun and the Earth visible from a lunar south pole landing site?

Team Albedo · NASA Space Apps 2026 · Challenge: Lunar Landing Site Navigator

Live demo · Code

Problem

  • Near the south pole the Sun skims the horizon, so terrain decides when a lander has power.
  • Earth is only sometimes in line of sight, which decides when it can talk to home.
  • Planners need both answers for a site and a date, in one place, in seconds: that is the rapid evaluation this challenge asks for.

Data

  • LRO LOLA elevation model, LDEM_85S_20M (PDS Geosciences Node).
  • JPL Horizons: hourly Sun and Earth azimuth/elevation for 2027.
  • NASA/LOLA-team average visibility maps, used to check our results.
  • Sites: Shackleton rim, Nobile rim, Malapert summit.

Method

  • Take a 64 km wide window around each site, resampled to 40 m (rays reach 30 km).
  • Ray-march a 360 degree horizon from a 2 m observer, with sphere curvature.
  • A body is visible when its elevation is above the horizon at its azimuth.
  • Output: illumination fraction, longest darkness, longest time without Earth.
